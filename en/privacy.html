Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Direkair-Direxyont Limited Partnership (“the shop”), 62/1-3 Sukhumvit Road, Bang Pla Soi, Mueang Chon Buri, Chon Buri 20000, is the data controller for personal data you give through www.direkair.com. This policy explains what we collect, what we use it for, how long we keep it, and your rights under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019).

1. What we collect

This website has no form for personal details, and doesn't collect your name, phone number or anything else that identifies you.

If you message the shop on LINE or call us, that information is held by LINE or your phone provider, not by this website. The shop uses it only to answer you and provide the service, never for marketing, and doesn't sell it or pass it to anyone else.

2. Visit statistics

The website counts visitors per day and how often the call, LINE and map buttons are tapped, so the shop can see how useful the website is. Only totals are kept; no IP addresses or anything that identifies you.

3. What's stored in your browser

The website keeps a few small values in your own browser (localStorage): the date of your last visit (so you're counted once a day), the site's colour theme, and announcements you've closed. These aren't sent to the shop and aren't advertising cookies.

The map (Google Maps) and fonts (Google Fonts) load from Google, which may receive technical data such as your IP address under Google's privacy policy.

4. Your rights

If you have questions about personal data, or want to see, correct or delete data the shop holds about you, or object to its use, you can do so at any time by contacting the shop on LINE or calling 038-285-061. You can also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee.
